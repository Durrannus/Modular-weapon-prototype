The game's fonts. All are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (free to use and ship in games).

- Galmuri9, Galmuri11 Bold and Galmuri7 (Lee Minseo, https://github.com/quiple/galmuri ; licence in Galmuri-OFL.md):
  the game's text since version 34. The files here hold only the Latin letters, digits and signs (the full fonts
  carry Korean and Japanese too, several megabytes each). Each is drawn only at its own size (10, 12 and 8) or a
  whole multiple of it: `Art.TEXT`, `Art.TEXT_BOLD`, `Art.TEXT_SMALL` in scripts/art.gd.
- Silkscreen (Google Fonts): the small capital headings and buttons, at 8 and its doubles.
- Pixelify Sans (Google Fonts): the text until version 33. No longer used: at the game's size (11) its digits
  came out smudged. Kept for now in case an old picture needs it.
